Service Overview

Commercial Roofing Tailored to Oakley Properties

Commercial roofs protect inventory, tenant operations, and mechanical equipment across Downtown Oakley, Main Street businesses, and East Contra Costa commercial corridors. Downtime from an active leak costs far more than proactive roof management.

All American Roofing works with building owners, property managers, and facility coordinators to inspect, repair, and replace commercial low-slope and steep-slope roofing systems with clear scheduling and minimal disruption to tenants and customers.

Common Problems Addressed by Commercial Roofing

HVAC Curb & Penetration Leaks

Rooftop service traffic and vibration frequently loosen pitch pans and curb flashing around AC units.

Clogged Scuppers & Ponding Water

Blocked drains create heavy standing water loads that degrade membrane seams.

Parapet Wall Coping Deterioration

Open sheet-metal coping joints let rainwater seep inside exterior stucco or masonry walls.

High Cooling Costs on Dark Roofs

Unreflective black roofs absorb intense summer heat, forcing commercial HVAC systems to work harder.
