Service Overview

Flat Roofing Tailored to Oakley Properties

Flat and low-slope roofs require completely different waterproofing physics than pitched shingle or tile roofs. Because rainwater moves slowly across low slopes, seams must be heat-welded or fully adhered—not merely overlapped—and drainage scuppers or internal drains must stay free of standing ponding water.

All American Roofing provides flat roof repair, replacement, and preventive care for Oakley commercial storefronts, offices, and residential flat roof sections or patio enclosures.

Common Problems Addressed by Flat Roofing

Ponding Water & Poor Slope Drainage

Low spots hold standing water after storms, accelerating membrane breakdown and stressing seams.

Open Lap Seams & Blisters

Aging torch-down or cap sheet seams pull apart under daily thermal expansion and contraction.

Parapet Wall & HVAC Curb Cracks

Flashings around rooftop AC units, skylights, and parapet coping are common sources of flat-roof leaks.

UV Chalking & Surface Cracking

Sun exposure dries older dark asphalt membranes unless protected by reflective cap sheets or coatings.

How We Work

Our Flat Roofing Process

01. Step

Slope & Drainage Survey

We inspect existing membrane condition, low ponding areas, parapet walls, and roof penetrations.

02. Step

Substrate & Taper Prep

Damaged decking or wet insulation is replaced; tapered insulation can improve positive drainage where needed.

03. Step

Membrane & Curb Flashing

TPO single-ply or modified bitumen membranes are installed with heat-welded seams and custom curb boots.

04. Step

Scupper & Edge Termination

Metal gravel stops, scuppers, and coping caps are sealed cleanly for watertight perimeter performance.

Materials & Scope Confirmation

Flat roof options include white reflective TPO membranes, multi-ply modified bitumen, and elastomeric roof coatings where the existing substrate qualifies. System selection is confirmed on-site.
